WALDEN: AI-Powered Educational Assistant Robot

Active Research

A character-based voice AI classroom assistant designed to democratize access to advanced learning technology for students in government schools.

Research Problem

Most educational robotics research focuses on expensive systems ($5,000-$30,000) or gives students unrestricted devices. There's limited exploration of affordable, education-restricted AI systems that work alongside teachers.

Innovation

WALDEN provides voice-based AI tutoring at $4.31 per student/year (including hardware amortization and API costs) — over 99% cheaper than commercial alternatives.

Conference Submission Update (October 2024)

Venue: AISTEMEDU 2025 (IEEE Conference)

Pilot Study: n=12 students, measuring engagement and system usability

Key Reviewer Feedback:

  • ✅ Strong technical merit and practical applicability
  • ✅ Cost analysis and hardware details valuable for replication
  • ⚠️ Need larger sample size with learning outcome measurements
  • ⚠️ Require statistical analysis and age-cohort comparisons
  • ⚠️ Address data privacy, offline fallback, and scalability

Action Taken: Designing expanded study (n=200) with pre/post testing, age-cohort analysis, and rigorous statistical methods.

Expanded Study Design (Jan 2025 Launch)

  • Sample: 200 students (ages 6-13), government schools in Hyderabad
  • Duration: 4-day intervention + 1-week retention testing
  • Measurements: Pre/post/retention MCQ tests (10 questions per cohort)
  • Design: Age-cohort groups (6-8 years, 9-13 years)
  • Additional Data: Engagement surveys, teacher feedback, system logs

Research Questions

  1. Can low-cost AI tutors achieve measurable learning gains comparable to expensive systems?
  2. Does character-based embodiment increase student engagement over text-based AI?
  3. Is education-restricted AI as effective as unrestricted AI for learning outcomes?
  4. How do age cohorts differ in interaction patterns and learning gains?

Planned Analysis

  • Paired t-tests (pre/post score comparisons within groups)
  • Cohen's d (effect size calculations for learning gains)
  • Mann-Whitney U tests (age-cohort comparisons)
  • Descriptive statistics (engagement metrics, response times)

Cost Breakdown

  • Hardware: ~$50 per robot (ESP32, microphone, speaker, servos, 3D printed parts)
  • API Costs: $2.65 per student/year (based on pilot usage patterns)
  • Infrastructure: Uses existing school WiFi (no additional costs)
  • Total: $4.31 per student/year vs. $5,000+ for commercial robots

Web Applications - Wheels N' Works System

Home Page

Landing page showcasing workshops, services, and instant booking functionality. Users can book appointments without creating an account, with social media integration and brand information easily accessible.

Non-Customer Management System

Guest booking system with auto-generated IDs. Non-customers receive appointment details via email including updates. System IDs are auto-generated and read-only. After invoicing, appointments become view-only.

Customer Management System

Registered customers can manage their profile, book appointments, and edit/delete current bookings. Once invoiced, appointments become view-only and invoices appear in customer profile for reference.

Shop Owner Admin System

Approved shop owners manage their shop info, services, appointments, and invoices. Can book appointments for customers, add service charges, and send/download invoices as PDF. After invoicing, only cost/payment updates allowed.

Super Admin Dashboard

Complete platform oversight: manage all shops (add/edit/delete owners), view system insights including total appointments, top services, top-earning shops, invoice counts, payment status, and non-customer bookings analytics.

Automated Email System

Email notifications for booking confirmations, admin registration, and invoice delivery. Demonstrates integration of automated communication system.
