🔍 Critical Gap Identified

Problem: Most existing research focuses on high-cost, laboratory-based social robots (e.g., NAO robots costing $5,000+). These systems are impractical for everyday classrooms and often attempt to replace the teacher, which can negatively influence students’ emotional engagement, reduce social interaction, and disrupt their sense of safety—particularly for students who are introverted or socially anxious.

Gap: There is limited research on low-cost, emotionally engaging, student-friendly educational robots that support teachers, maintain a safe classroom environment, and promote positive affective connections without replacing human interaction.

Solution direction: Develop an educational robot that integrates affordability, affective interaction, and pedagogical effectiveness — a cute, classroom-friendly assistant designed to enhance teacher–student engagement while preserving emotional safety and comfort.

📊 Key Findings

  • Robotics improves engagement and cognitive skills
  • Evidence supports measurable learning outcomes
  • High costs limit adoption in real classrooms
  • Strong need for affordable alternatives
